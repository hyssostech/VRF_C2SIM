# DRAFT - MAK support case: VR-Forces 5.2d back end stops on a repeated navigate-to-location (NOT SENT)

STATUS: DRAFT for the operator. NOTHING has been sent to MAK. Contacting the vendor is the operator's decision.
Record: docs/experiments/REPRO_BACKEND_STOP_2026-10-15.md (and D36 / D47 for the history). Customer data: NONE in the
repro below. The only real-world data are two coordinate pairs (a place on the public MAK Earth terrain) - see "Is the
location needed?".

## Summary (proposed case text)

Product: VR-Forces 5.2d (vrfSimHLA1516e.exe, HLA 1516e, 64-bit), MAK RTI 5.0.1, VR-Link 5.10, Windows 11 Pro.
Terrain: MAK Earth Aggregate (online) .mtf. Simulation model set: AggregateTacticalLevel. Back end and front end
(vrfGui) on one host; the tasks are issued by a remote-controller application (our C2SIM interface) through
DtVrfRemoteController.

What happens: the sim engine (back end) stops simulating within ~0.5 s of a second navigate-to-location being issued to
the subordinates of an aggregate. It does not crash: no callstack, no dump, no dialog; the process stays up; its log
stops being written; one to three threads run at 100% CPU indefinitely; its working set stays flat. Every remote
controller then extrapolates the simulation clock at 1x and, ~2 min later, ages the back end out of its back-end list.
The back end refuses a graceful close (console exit / taskkill without /F) and has to be terminated.

Reproduced 5 times out of 6 at one location (once with a fully synthetic unit), 0 times out of 2 at another location.

## Steps to reproduce (vendor-level)

1. Load a scenario on MAK Earth Aggregate (online), model set AggregateTacticalLevel. Run (we ran fixed-frame, run to
   complete; the sim ran at ~5-10x real time).
2. Create a US infantry brigade AGGREGATE (container), entity type 11:1:225:8:3:1:1, at 54.0194 N 23.3139 E.
3. Create 17 subordinate aggregates AGGREGATED and attach them to it (AddToOrganization), on a ring of ~490 m around
   its point: 4 x "Stryker HHT (USA)", 12 x "Mech CO (USA, M2)", 1 x "Stryker Cavalry SQDN (USA)".
4. Send to EACH of the 17 subordinates the scripted task navigate-to-location with destination 54.0403 N 23.3364 E,
   obstacleQuery "MAK_OBSTACLE", pathQuery "MAK_ROAD", buffer 10, displayRoute false, query "".
5. About 60-90 s (wall) later - while most subordinates are still moving; in one run after all 17 had completed -
   send the SAME task with the SAME destination to all 17 again.
6. Observe: within 0.3-0.5 s the back end's log stops and the back end no longer advances; its threads spin.
Control: the same steps with the destination 54.000 N 23.290 E (a different, dry location) - no hang (2 of 2).

Observed in every hang: the back-end log's last lines are member re-plans ("Graph search phase starting", "Obstacle
avoidance phase starting", and bursts of "Failed to navigate off road: Start point is within obstacle" - except one
hang that logged none of the latter). The first move's route crosses OSM water (a lake) ~0.75 km from the start.

## Is the location needed?

YES, as far as we can tell: the same task sequence at a different place did not hang (2 of 2), and at this place it hung
5 of 6 times. The coordinates (54.0194 N 23.3139 E start, 54.0403 N 23.3364 E destination) are a lake district on the
public MAK Earth terrain; they describe no unit, organisation or plan. Whether to share them is the operator's call.
